## Engine features
- [x] Debts paid every two weeks (Split): `Debt.biweeklyFrom`.
- [x] Irregular income: "live on last month's money" — earnings come in as inflows tied to the income; the 1st of the month pays what came last month.
- [x] Pay ahead: a bigger pay can keep the extra for the next payday (`Payday.keep`).
- [x] Payday recap: what the last period did (saved, paid, left, goals moved).
- [x] Runway: months / days your savings carry you without pay; nudge to 3–6 months.
- [x] Net worth: savings + other assets − debts, month by month (`Asset`).
- [x] Who owes whom in a couple: shared costs paid from one person's account; "settled" marks.
- [x] Subscription review every 3 months ("still using it?") + reminder.
- [x] What-if: salary change, a new monthly cost or saving, months without pay → a day, free money, runway.
